# Data Retention Policy

**Last Updated:** January 1, 2024

## 1. Purpose

This Data Retention Policy defines how long we retain different types of data and the procedures for data deletion. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and protect our legitimate business interests.

## 2. General Principles

### 2.1 Retention Principles

We follow these core principles:
- **Necessity:** Retain data only when necessary
- **Proportionality:** Retention periods proportionate to purpose
- **Legality:** Comply with legal requirements
- **Security:** Protect data throughout retention period
- **Transparency:** Communicate retention periods clearly

### 2.2 Factors Determining Retention

Retention periods are based on:
- Service provision needs
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Contractual obligations
- Legitimate business purposes
- User expectations
- Industry best practices

## 3. Data Retention Periods

### 3.1 Account Data

**Active Accounts:**
- **Retention:** While account is active and for service provision
- **Includes:** Profile information, preferences, settings
- **Review:** Annually for inactive accounts

**Closed/Deleted Accounts:**
- **Deletion:** 30 days after account closure
- **Backup retention:** 90 days
- **Exceptions:** Data retained for legal compliance

### 3.2 Identity and Authentication Data

**User Credentials:**
- **Retention:** While account exists
- **Deletion:** 30 days after account closure
- **Password history:** 12 months (last 5 passwords)

**Authentication Logs:**
- **Retention:** 90 days
- **Purpose:** Security monitoring and incident investigation
- **Exception:** Fraud investigation may require longer retention

### 3.3 Financial and Billing Data

**Invoices and Payment Records:**
- **Retention:** 7 years from transaction date
- **Purpose:** Tax compliance, accounting, audit requirements
- **Legal basis:** Federal and state tax laws

**Payment Methods:**
- **Active methods:** While account is active
- **Deleted methods:** Removed immediately from active systems
- **Transaction history:** 7 years

**Tax Documents:**
- **Retention:** 7 years
- **Purpose:** Tax compliance and audit defense

### 3.4 User Content

**Active User Content:**
- **Retention:** While account is active
- **User control:** Users can delete content at any time
- **Deletion:** Immediately from active systems

**Deleted User Content:**
- **Active systems:** Deleted within 72 hours
- **Backup systems:** Deleted within 90 days
- **Exception:** Content shared with other users may persist

**Collaborative Content:**
- **Retention:** As long as any collaborator's account is active
- **Deletion:** When all collaborators delete or account closes

### 3.5 Usage and Analytics Data

**Usage Logs:**
- **Detailed logs:** 90 days
- **Aggregated statistics:** 2 years
- **Purpose:** Service improvement and analytics

**Analytics Data:**
- **Individual-level:** 18 months
- **Aggregated data:** Indefinitely (anonymized)
- **Purpose:** Product development and research

**API Logs:**
- **Request logs:** 90 days
- **Error logs:** 180 days
- **Purpose:** Debugging and monitoring

### 3.6 Communications

**Customer Support:**
- **Email correspondence:** 3 years
- **Chat transcripts:** 3 years
- **Phone recordings:** 90 days (unless needed for dispute)
- **Purpose:** Service quality, training, dispute resolution

**Marketing Communications:**
- **Email lists:** While consent is active
- **Campaign data:** 3 years
- **Opt-out records:** Indefinitely (to honor opt-outs)

**Transactional Emails:**
- **Retention:** 1 year
- **Purpose:** Service records and support

### 3.7 Legal and Compliance Data

**Legal Requests:**
- **Retention:** 7 years after resolution
- **Purpose:** Legal defense and compliance

**Compliance Records:**
- **Audit records:** 7 years
- **Compliance certifications:** 7 years
- **Risk assessments:** 3 years

**Litigation Hold:**
- **Duration:** Until legal matter is resolved
- **Scope:** All relevant data preserved
- **Override:** Supersedes standard retention periods

### 3.8 Security and Incident Data

**Security Logs:**
- **Access logs:** 1 year
- **Security events:** 3 years
- **Purpose:** Security monitoring and forensics

**Incident Records:**
- **Data breaches:** 5 years
- **Security incidents:** 3 years
- **Investigation records:** 3 years

### 3.9 Employee and Internal Data

**Employee Records:**
- **Current employees:** Duration of employment
- **Former employees:** 7 years after termination
- **Purpose:** Employment law compliance

**Internal Communications:**
- **Operational:** 3 years
- **Strategic:** 7 years
- **Purpose:** Business records and compliance

### 3.10 Backup and Disaster Recovery

**System Backups:**
- **Incremental:** 30 days
- **Full backups:** 90 days
- **Purpose:** Disaster recovery
- **Note:** Deleted data may persist in backups until backup expires

**Archived Data:**
- **Long-term archives:** Per category retention period
- **Access:** Restricted, logged
- **Review:** Annual assessment of necessity

## 4. Data Deletion Procedures

### 4.1 Automated Deletion

We implement automated processes to:
- Delete data when retention period expires
- Remove data from backup rotations
- Purge temporary files and caches
- Archive or delete inactive accounts

### 4.2 Secure Deletion Methods

**Active Systems:**
- Data overwritten multiple times
- Database records permanently deleted
- File system secure deletion

**Backup Systems:**
- Data removed when backup expires
- Backup media securely destroyed

**Hardware Disposal:**
- Secure data wiping (DoD 5220.22-M standard)
- Physical destruction of storage media
- Certificate of destruction obtained

### 4.3 Verification

Deletion processes include:
- Automated verification checks
- Audit logging of deletions
- Quarterly verification audits
- Annual comprehensive review

## 5. Exceptions to Retention Periods

### 5.1 Legal Holds

Data subject to legal proceedings is retained until:
- Legal matter is resolved
- Statute of limitations expires
- Court authorizes disposal

### 5.2 Regulatory Investigations

Data involved in regulatory investigations is retained until:
- Investigation is closed
- Regulatory requirements are satisfied
- Appeal periods expire

### 5.3 Ongoing Disputes

Data related to disputes is retained until:
- Dispute is resolved
- Settlement is finalized
- Appeal periods expire

### 5.4 Fraud Prevention

Data related to fraud or security incidents may be retained longer for:
- Investigation purposes
- Prevention of future incidents
- Law enforcement cooperation

## 6. Extended Retention Requests

### 6.1 User Requests

Users may request extended retention for:
- Legal purposes
- Regulatory requirements
- Business needs

**Process:**
- Submit request to legal@company.com
- Provide justification
- Specify retention period needed
- Annual review required

### 6.2 Legal Requirements

We honor requests from:
- Law enforcement (with proper legal process)
- Regulatory authorities
- Courts and legal proceedings

## 7. Data Minimization

We continuously work to minimize data retention:

**Regular Reviews:**
- Quarterly assessment of retention needs
- Annual comprehensive policy review
- Evaluation of new technologies

**Data Reduction:**
- Anonymization where possible
- Pseudonymization techniques
- Aggregation of personal data

**Archival:**
- Move inactive data to cold storage
- Implement access controls
- Maintain data integrity

## 8. User Control

### 8.1 Access and Export

Users can:
- Access their data anytime
- Export data in standard formats
- Request deletion of specific data

### 8.2 Deletion Requests

Users can request deletion:
- Via account settings
- By emailing privacy@company.com
- Through support channels

**Processing time:** 30 days maximum

**Exceptions communicated:**
- Legal obligations
- Ongoing disputes
- Fraud prevention

## 9. Breach of Retention Policy

Violations of this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action for employees
- Audit and investigation
- Remedial measures
- Regulatory reporting if required

## 10. Third-Party Data Processors

We ensure processors:
- Follow equivalent retention policies
- Delete data upon instruction
- Provide deletion confirmation
- Maintain audit records

## 11. Policy Updates

This policy is reviewed and updated:
- Annually at minimum
- When laws or regulations change
- When business practices change
- After significant incidents

Users are notified of material changes.
