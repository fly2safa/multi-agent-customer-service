## 2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

### 2.1 Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b))

Processing necessary to:
- Provide the Service you requested
- Process payments
- Manage your account
- Fulfill our contractual obligations

**Data types:** Account information, billing data, service usage

### 2.2 Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f))

Processing for legitimate business purposes:
- Improve and develop the Service
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Ensure security
- Direct marketing to existing customers
- Business analytics

**Balancing test:** We have assessed that our legitimate interests do not override your rights and freedoms.

**Data types:** Usage analytics, log data, performance metrics

### 2.3 Consent (Art. 6(1)(a))

Processing based on your explicit consent:
- Marketing communications to prospects
- Non-essential cookies
- Optional features
- Third-party data sharing

**Withdrawal:** You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

### 2.4 Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

Processing required by law:
- Tax reporting
- Financial record keeping
- Response to legal requests
- Regulatory compliance

**Data types:** Financial records, legal communications

## 3. Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

### 3.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)

You can request:
- Confirmation of data processing
- Copy of your personal data
- Information about processing purposes and recipients

**Response time:** 30 days  
**Method:** Email privacy@company.com or use in-app tool

### 3.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

You can request correction of:
- Inaccurate personal data
- Incomplete personal data

**Response time:** 30 days  
**Method:** Account Settings or contact privacy@company.com

### 3.3 Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17)

You can request deletion when:
- Data no longer necessary for original purpose
- You withdraw consent
- You object to processing
- Data was unlawfully processed
- Legal obligation requires deletion

**Exceptions:** We may retain data when:
- Necessary for legal compliance
- Required for legal claims
- Exercise of freedom of expression
- Public interest purposes

**Response time:** 30 days  
**Method:** Account Settings > Delete Account or email privacy@company.com

### 3.4 Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18)

You can request restriction when:
- Accuracy of data is contested
- Processing is unlawful but you oppose deletion
- We no longer need data but you need it for legal claims
- You object to processing pending verification

**Response time:** 30 days  
**Method:** Email privacy@company.com

### 3.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

You can:
- Receive your data in structured, machine-readable format
- Transmit data to another controller

**Formats available:** CSV, JSON  
**Method:** Account Settings > Export Data

### 3.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)

You can object to processing based on:
- Legitimate interests
- Direct marketing (absolute right)
- Scientific/historical research
- Statistical purposes

**Response time:** 30 days  
**Method:** Email privacy@company.com or use opt-out links

### 3.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22)

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing with legal or significant effects.

**Our practice:** We do not make fully automated decisions that significantly affect you without human review.

### 3.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You can file a complaint with:
- Your local supervisory authority
- The Irish Data Protection Commission (our lead authority)

## 4. Data Protection Principles

We adhere to GDPR's core principles:

### 4.1 Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency

- We process data lawfully with valid legal basis
- We are transparent about data processing
- We provide clear privacy information

### 4.2 Purpose Limitation

- We collect data for specified, explicit purposes
- We do not process data for incompatible purposes
- We inform you of any purpose changes

### 4.3 Data Minimization

- We collect only necessary data
- We regularly review data needs
- We delete unnecessary data

### 4.4 Accuracy

- We maintain accurate and up-to-date data
- We enable you to correct information
- We correct inaccuracies promptly

### 4.5 Storage Limitation

- We retain data only as long as necessary
- We have defined retention periods
- We delete data when no longer needed

### 4.6 Integrity and Confidentiality

- We implement appropriate security measures
- We protect against unauthorized access
- We train staff on data protection

### 4.7 Accountability

- We demonstrate compliance
- We maintain processing records
- We conduct data protection impact assessments

We conduct DPIAs for:
- New technologies
- Large-scale processing
- Systematic monitoring
- Sensitive data processing

**DPIA Process:**
1. Describe processing operations
2. Assess necessity and proportionality
3. Identify risks to rights and freedoms
4. Evaluate mitigation measures
5. Consult DPO
6. Seek supervisory authority input (if high risk remains)
