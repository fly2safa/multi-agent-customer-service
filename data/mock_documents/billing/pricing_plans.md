# Pricing Plans

## Overview
Our service offers flexible pricing plans designed to meet the needs of businesses of all sizes.

## Starter Plan - $29/month
Perfect for small businesses and startups.

**Features:**
- Up to 5 users
- 10 GB storage
- Basic support (email only)
- 99.5% uptime SLA
- API access: 1,000 requests/day
- Monthly invoicing

## Professional Plan - $99/month
Ideal for growing teams and businesses.

**Features:**
- Up to 25 users
- 100 GB storage
- Priority support (email + chat)
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- API access: 10,000 requests/day
- Advanced analytics
- Monthly or annual invoicing
- Custom integrations

## Enterprise Plan - $499/month
Comprehensive solution for large organizations.

**Features:**
- Unlimited users
- 1 TB storage
- Premium 24/7 support (phone + email + chat)
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- API access: Unlimited
- Advanced analytics + custom reports
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom integrations + SSO
- Quarterly business reviews
- Annual invoicing with custom terms

## Add-ons
- Additional storage: $5/GB per month
- Extra API requests: $10 per 1,000 requests
- Advanced security features: $50/month
- Training sessions: $200 per session

## Payment Terms
All plans are billed monthly in advance unless annual billing is selected. Annual plans receive a 15% discount.
