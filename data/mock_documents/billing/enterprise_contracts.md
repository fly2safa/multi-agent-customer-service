# Enterprise Contract Terms

## Overview
Enterprise plans include custom contract terms tailored to your organization's needs.

## Standard Contract Terms

### Contract Duration
- Minimum commitment: 12 months
- Auto-renewal: Yes, with 90-day notice period for cancellation
- Early termination fee: 50% of remaining contract value

### Payment Terms
- Net 30 payment terms available
- Annual payment: Full payment due upon contract signing
- Quarterly payment: Available with credit approval, due at start of each quarter
- Purchase orders accepted

### Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Uptime Guarantee:** 99.99%
- Monthly uptime calculation excludes scheduled maintenance
- Credits issued for downtime exceeding SLA:
  - 99.9% - 99.99%: 10% credit
  - 99.0% - 99.9%: 25% credit
  - Below 99.0%: 50% credit

**Support Response Times:**
- Critical issues (system down): 1 hour
- High priority: 4 hours
- Medium priority: 8 hours
- Low priority: 24 hours

### Data and Security
- Data encryption in transit and at rest (AES-256)
- SOC 2 Type II compliance
- Annual security audits included
- Dedicated security contact
- Custom data retention policies available

### Customization Options
- Custom API rate limits
- Dedicated infrastructure (additional cost)
- Custom integrations (scoped separately)
- White-labeling available
- Custom SSO/SAML configuration

## Volume Discounts

Discounts available based on user count:
- 50-100 users: 10% discount
- 101-250 users: 15% discount
- 251-500 users: 20% discount
- 501+ users: Custom pricing (contact sales)

## Professional Services

Enterprise customers have access to professional services:
- Implementation and onboarding: Included
- Custom training sessions: 4 sessions included annually
- Dedicated account manager: Included
- Technical account manager: Available (additional cost)
- Custom development: Quoted separately

## Contract Modifications

Contract amendments require:
- Written request from authorized signatory
- Mutual agreement on terms
- Updated SOW or amendment document
- 30-day notice period for pricing changes

## Renewal Terms

- Renewal pricing locked for contract duration
- Price increases limited to 5% per year at renewal
- 90-day notice required for non-renewal
- Auto-renewal unless written notice provided

## Termination

Customers may terminate for:
- Material breach by provider (30-day cure period)
- Bankruptcy or insolvency
- Consistent SLA violations

Early termination requires:
- Written notice
- Payment of early termination fee
- 60-day wind-down period
- Data export assistance

## Support Escalation

Enterprise customers have direct access to:
- Dedicated Slack channel
- Phone support (24/7)
- Video conferencing for critical issues
- Executive escalation path
