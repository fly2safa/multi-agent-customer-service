# Invoice and Payment Policies

## Invoicing Schedule

### Monthly Plans
Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month for the upcoming billing period. Payment is due within 7 days of invoice date.

### Annual Plans
Invoices are generated 30 days before the renewal date. Payment is due within 14 days of invoice date.

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- ACH bank transfers (US only)
- Wire transfers (for Enterprise customers)
- PayPal (Starter and Professional plans only)

## Viewing Your Invoices

1. Log into your account dashboard
2. Navigate to "Billing" > "Invoices"
3. Click on any invoice to view details or download PDF
4. Past invoices are available for up to 7 years

## Late Payment Policy

**Grace Period:** 7 days after due date with no penalty

**After Grace Period:**
- Service may be suspended after 14 days
- Late fee of 1.5% per month on outstanding balance
- Account reactivation fee: $25 (waived for first occurrence)

## Refund Policy

- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee** for new customers on annual plans
- Pro-rated refunds for downgrades (credit applied to next invoice)
- No refunds on monthly plans after service has been activated
- Add-ons are non-refundable

## Tax Information

- Sales tax is applied based on your billing address
- International customers may be subject to VAT
- Tax exemption certificates must be submitted before invoice generation

## Disputes

If you believe there is an error on your invoice, please contact our billing department within 30 days at billing@company.com.
