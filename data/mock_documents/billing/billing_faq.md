# Billing Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### How do I upgrade my plan?
You can upgrade your plan at any time from your account dashboard. Go to Billing > Plans and select your new plan. You'll be charged a pro-rated amount for the remainder of your billing period.

### How do I downgrade my plan?
Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Go to Billing > Plans, select the plan you want to downgrade to, and confirm. You'll receive a pro-rated credit on your next invoice.

### Can I cancel at any time?
Yes, you can cancel at any time. Monthly plans can be canceled with no penalty. Annual plans may be subject to early termination fees unless canceled during the 30-day money-back guarantee period.

### What happens if I cancel mid-month?
Your service will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No pro-rated refunds are issued for monthly plans.

## Invoice Questions

### When will I receive my invoice?
Monthly invoices are sent on the 1st of each month. Annual invoices are sent 30 days before your renewal date.

### Can I get a copy of past invoices?
Yes! Log into your account and go to Billing > Invoices. You can view and download all past invoices.

### Can I change my billing email?
Yes, go to Account Settings > Billing Information and update your billing email address.

### Do you offer purchase orders?
Yes, purchase orders are available for Enterprise customers only. Contact your account manager to set this up.

## Payment Questions

### When is payment due?
Payment is due 7 days from the invoice date for monthly plans and 14 days for annual plans.

### What happens if my payment fails?
We'll notify you immediately and retry the payment after 3 days. If it fails again, we'll retry after 7 days. Please update your payment method to avoid service interruption.

### Can I pay annually instead of monthly?
Yes! Annual payment is available for all plans and includes a 15% discount. Contact support to switch to annual billing.

### Do you accept PayPal?
PayPal is accepted for Starter and Professional plans only. Enterprise customers must use credit card, ACH, or wire transfer.

## Credit and Refund Questions

### How do credits work?
Credits are applied automatically to your next invoice. They appear in your account balance and never expire.

### When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days and will appear on your original payment method within 10 business days.

### Can I get a refund for unused time?
Monthly plans are not eligible for pro-rated refunds. Annual plans are eligible for pro-rated refunds if canceled within the first 30 days.

## Tax Questions

### Why was I charged sales tax?
Sales tax is applied based on your billing address and local tax regulations.

### How do I submit a tax exemption certificate?
Email your tax exemption certificate to billing@company.com. It must be received before your next invoice is generated.

### Can I get a W-9 form?
Yes, you can download our W-9 form from the Billing section or request it via email at billing@company.com.
