# Payment Methods Guide

## Adding a Payment Method

### Credit/Debit Card
1. Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods
2. Click "Add Payment Method"
3. Enter card details (number, expiration, CVV)
4. Add billing address
5. Click "Save"

### Bank Account (ACH)
Available for Professional and Enterprise plans only.

1. Navigate to Payment Methods
2. Select "Add Bank Account"
3. Enter routing number and account number
4. Verify with micro-deposits (takes 1-2 business days)
5. Confirm verification amounts

### Wire Transfer
Available for Enterprise customers only. Contact your account manager for wire transfer instructions.

## Setting Default Payment Method

Your default payment method will be charged automatically for recurring invoices:
1. Go to Payment Methods
2. Click the three dots next to desired method
3. Select "Set as Default"

## Updating Payment Information

To update an existing payment method:
1. Go to Payment Methods
2. Click "Edit" next to the method you want to update
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save Changes"

Note: You cannot edit a card number. You must add a new card and delete the old one.

## Removing a Payment Method

1. Go to Payment Methods
2. Click "Remove" next to the method
3. Confirm deletion

Note: You must have at least one payment method on file. Add a new method before removing your last one.

## Automatic Billing

All plans are set to automatic billing by default. Your default payment method will be charged:
- Monthly plans: 1st of each month
- Annual plans: On your anniversary date

To disable automatic billing, contact support. Note: This may result in service interruption if invoices are not paid manually.

## Payment Security

- All payment information is encrypted with 256-bit SSL
- We are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant
- Card details are never stored on our servers (tokenized via Stripe)
- Two-factor authentication available for billing changes

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails:
1. You'll receive an email notification immediately
2. System will retry in 3 days
3. Second retry in 7 days
4. After second failure, account may be suspended

Update your payment method immediately to avoid service interruption.
